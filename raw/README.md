# raw/

The inbox. Anything worth keeping lands here first, unfiltered:
`scripts/capture.sh "text or link"`. Frontmatter: `created`, `status`
(`unprocessed` | `processed`) and `distilled_into` (slugs of the notes it
became).

Most captures never become a note, and that's fine; the `distill` skill
decides. Binary originals belong in `raw/<capture>/originals/` or
`raw/<capture>/images/`.
